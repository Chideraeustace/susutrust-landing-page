Common Questions

Do I need a smartphone or internet to use SusuTrust?

No. SusuTrust runs entirely over USSD — the same code-dialling menus you use to check your MoMo balance. It works on any phone, on any of the supported networks, with zero data.

What happens if I miss a contribution?

If an automatic collection fails, you and your group leader are notified immediately by SMS and your TrustScore drops by 10 points. Fall below 70 and you're automatically removed from the circle so the rest of the group isn't put at risk.

Who decides the order people get paid in?

Your TrustScore does. Members with a higher score from consistent, on-time payments are prioritised for payout. If two members are tied, whoever joined the group first goes first.

Can I choose to pay manually instead of automatic deductions?

Yes. When you join a group you choose Automatic (SusuTrust requests your MoMo PIN on schedule) or Manual (you dial in and pay it yourself each round) — you're not locked into one style.

Which mobile networks are supported?

MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo Money are all supported for both contributions and payouts.
